## The 9 questions (from the brief, abridged)

1. **Your definition.** What is a software factory to you; where does it sit between "tasks in, shipped changes out" and "how agents talk to each other"; what is it deliberately not?
2. **Problems and people.** Which problems does it solve, for whom, in what order; which would you leave alone, and why?
3. **The design.** The system at the level of detail that matters; walk through one real change from idea to production (what agents do, where people step in); zoom into the most interesting or risky part.
4. **The people using it.** Day to day per user: where they start, how they steer, review, why they trust it, what happens when it goes wrong, how you know it works for them.
5. **Delegate, review, own.** What goes to agents, what a human must review, what a human must own, and why the line sits there.
6. **Quality, security and governance.** Correct and secure output in a regulated environment; quality beyond "it looks right"; authorship, approval and audit.
7. **Apps built by non-engineers.** How the operations analyst ships something useful, safely and within governance, without becoming an engineer.
8. **Adoption without a mandate.** Winning over the senior engineer; bringing the early adopter's habits up to standard.
9. **Where you would start.** The first 90 days and what you expect to learn.
